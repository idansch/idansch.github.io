Pit.Kit Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how IS Apps (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use Pit.Kit. Pit.Kit is designed to keep your chats and follow-through tools private and local first.

Your Pit.Kit Content

Chats, messages, tags, reminders, audio, photos, videos, documents, Custom Entries, and related metadata are stored on your device. Pit.Kit does not sell your content, use it for advertising, or use it to train advertising profiles.

If you enable private iCloud sync or backups, Pit.Kit sends the applicable content to your private Apple iCloud container so it can sync or be recovered on your devices. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

Notty and Local AI

Pit.Kit’s documented Notty and local-AI features are designed to process the notes and context they need on-device. Pit.Kit does not send note text, attachments, prompts, model responses, chat names, tags, reminder dates, or document contents to analytics or crash-reporting services.

If a future version introduces a feature that sends your content to a new external AI service, we will describe that feature and its data handling before it is used.

Diagnostics and Product Analytics

We use Mixpanel for limited product analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for crash monitoring. These services may receive an anonymous installation identifier and technical information such as app version, device and operating-system information, and crash diagnostics. Pit.Kit limits product events to categorical information, such as a feature type or outcome; they do not include the contents of your notes or attachments.

Pit.Kit Plus and Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple’s App Store. We use RevenueCat to help provide subscription status, restore purchases, and manage access to Pit.Kit Plus. RevenueCat may process technical and transaction information needed to verify your entitlement, including an app-specific identifier and App Store purchase information. Apple processes payment details; we do not receive your full payment-card number.

When Information Is Shared

We share information only with service providers needed to operate the relevant feature: Apple for optional iCloud and App Store services, Mixpanel for limited analytics, Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics, and RevenueCat for subscriptions. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect people, our rights, or the security of the service.

Retention, Export, and Deletion

Your Pit.Kit content remains on your device until you delete it, remove the app or its data, or otherwise manage it through the app. You can export your chat history through Pit.Kit’s storage tools. Deleting local content does not automatically delete a separate iCloud backup; manage iCloud storage and backups through Pit.Kit and your Apple Account settings.

Analytics and purchase information are retained by the applicable provider under its own retention practices. You may contact us to request help with a privacy question or applicable privacy right.

Children, Changes, and Contact

Pit.Kit is not directed to children under 13. We may update this Policy as Pit.Kit changes; the updated version will show a new effective date. For questions, contact privacy@isapps.app or support@isapps.app.
