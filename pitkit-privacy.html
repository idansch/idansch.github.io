Pit.Kit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Who We Are and What This Policy Covers

IS Apps (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates Pit.Kit. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use Pit.Kit, visit this website, or contact us about the app. Pit.Kit is designed to keep your chats and follow-through tools private and local first.

Your Pit.Kit Content

Chats, messages, tags, reminders, audio, photos, videos, documents, Custom Entries, and related metadata are stored on your device. Pit.Kit does not sell your content, use it for advertising, or use it to train advertising profiles.

If you choose to enable iCloud sync or backups, applicable content is stored in your private Apple iCloud container so that it can sync or be recovered on your devices. IS Apps does not operate a service that receives or stores the contents of your Pit.Kit chats, attachments, or Custom Entries as part of normal app operation. Apple handles iCloud under its own terms and privacy policy.

Notty and Local AI

On supported devices, Notty and Pit.Kit’s local-AI features process the notes and context they need on your device. Pit.Kit does not send note text, attachments, prompts, model responses, chat names, tags, reminder dates, or document contents to analytics or crash-reporting services.

If a future feature would send your Pit.Kit content to an external AI service, we will explain what data would be sent, name the service, explain why it is needed, and obtain any consent required before the feature sends that content. This Policy alone is not consent for such a future feature.

Diagnostics, Analytics, and Support

We use Mixpanel for limited product analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for crash monitoring. These services may process device or app identifiers, app version, device and operating-system information, diagnostic information, and high-level feature-use or outcome information. They do not receive the contents of your notes or attachments.

If you contact us, we process the contact details and message you choose to provide so that we can respond and provide support. Please do not send private Pit.Kit content to us unless it is necessary for your request.

Pit.Kit Pro and Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple’s App Store. We use RevenueCat to help provide subscription status, restore purchases, and manage access to Pit.Kit Pro. RevenueCat may process technical and transaction information needed to verify your entitlement, including an app-specific identifier and App Store purchase information. Apple processes payment details; we do not receive your full payment-card number.

How Information Is Used and Shared

We use the limited information described above to provide and support Pit.Kit, maintain security and reliability, understand high-level product performance, and manage Pit.Kit Pro purchases. We share information only with service providers needed for those purposes: Apple for iCloud and App Store services, Mixpanel for analytics, Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics, and RevenueCat for purchases. We may also disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect people, our rights, or the security of the service.

We do not sell personal information or use Pit.Kit content for targeted advertising. We require service providers acting on our behalf to handle personal information in accordance with applicable law and their contractual data-protection commitments.

Retention, Export, Deletion, and Your Choices

Your Pit.Kit content remains on your device until you delete it, remove the app or its data, or otherwise manage it through the app. You can export your chat history through Pit.Kit’s storage tools. Deleting local content does not automatically delete an existing iCloud backup; manage iCloud storage and backups through Pit.Kit and your Apple Account settings.

We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain a record of the request, or meet legal obligations. Analytics, diagnostic, and purchase-related information is retained according to the applicable provider’s retention settings and practices. Where a choice or consent is required for analytics under applicable law, we will seek it before collection and honor a withdrawal of that choice.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing. To make a request or withdraw a privacy choice, email privacy@isapps.app. We may need to verify your request and may be unable to action information that we cannot identify or access, such as content held only in your private iCloud container.

Age Rating, International Processing, Changes, and Contact

Pit.Kit is designed with adult users in mind. It does not independently verify a user’s age or apply an age gate. The App Store age rating and availability shown to you are determined for your country or region. If we learn that we have collected personal information in a way that requires parental consent, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

Our service providers may process limited technical, diagnostic, support, or purchase information in countries other than yours. Where applicable, we use the safeguards required for those transfers.

We may update this Policy as Pit.Kit changes. For material changes, we will provide notice where required by law; the updated version will show a new effective date. For privacy questions, contact privacy@isapps.app. For product support, contact support@isapps.app.
