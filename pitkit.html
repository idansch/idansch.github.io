Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Pit.Kit, privacy, and where to get help.

When will Pit.Kit be available?

Pit.Kit is coming soon. This page will be updated as availability details are ready to share.

What can I keep in Pit.Kit?

Pit.Kit is designed for ideas, notes, media, and documents, kept together in focused chats.

Where does my content live?

Your content is stored on your device. You can optionally use private iCloud sync and recovery backups for your own devices.

Does Pit.Kit use my notes for advertising?

No. Pit.Kit does not sell your content, use it for advertising, or use it to train advertising profiles.

What is Notty?

Notty is the Pit.Kit assistant for private help and grounded recall from the conversation you are already having.

Is Pit.Kit an emergency or professional advice service?

No. Pit.Kit helps you capture information and manage reminders; it is not a medical, legal, financial, emergency, or safety service.
