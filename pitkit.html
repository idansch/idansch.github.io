Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Pit.Kit, privacy, and where to get help.

When will Pit.Kit be available?

Pit.Kit is available now on the App Store.

What can I keep in Pit.Kit?

Pit.Kit is designed for ideas, notes, media, and documents, kept together in focused chats. Pro users can also create custom entry types for advanced use cases

Where does my content live?

Your content is ONLY yours and stored on your device at any time. You can optionally use private iCloud sync and recovery backups for your own devices.

Does Pit.Kit use my notes for advertising?

No. Never. We do not sell your content, use it for advertising, or use it to train a model. Your data is yours.

What is Notty?

Notty is the Pit.Kit assistant for private help and grounded recall from the conversation you are already having. It runs locally on device and only for capable devices. Otherwise it'll provide basic app help information

Is Pit.Kit an emergency or professional advice service?

No. Pit.Kit helps you capture information and manage reminders; it is not a medical, legal, financial, emergency, or safety service. It is a plainly a notes and reminders tool.
