Pit.Kit

Pit.Kit is a private, chat-native space to capture thoughts quickly and follow through when it matters.

Capture, then follow through

  • Keep ideas, notes, media, and documents together in focused chats.
  • Turn a thought into a reminder when you are ready, with quick actions for the moments in between.
  • Ask Notty for private help and grounded recall from the conversation you are already having.

Your notes stay local first, with optional private iCloud sync and recovery backups.
